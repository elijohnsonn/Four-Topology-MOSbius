Device Declaration for C1 Four-Topology MOSbius
================================================

Primitive devices used:
- nfet_03v3 (3.3V NMOS transistor)
- pfet_03v3 (3.3V PMOS transistor)
- cap_nmos_03v3_b (3.3V NMOS capacitor with bulk tie)
- diode_pd2nw_06v0 (P-diffusion to N-well diode, 6V)
- diode_nd2ps_06v0 (N-diffusion to P-sub diode, 6V)
- ppolyf_u (Unsilicided P-poly resistor)

All devices are from the GF180MCU open-source PDK (gf180mcuD).
